How To Start

plugin allows you to add your entire catalogue to your WordPress website seamlessly. The plugin automatically imports and syncs all the product information.

First of all the plugin needs the connector ID code and the private key. You will find them in the connector details of .

How to add a connector
  1. Go to → Connectors and click the Add connector button.
  2. Enter the connection credentials in the modal and confirm.
  3. Once the connector appears in the list, push Synchronize to import categories, products and product variants automatically.
Requirements for synchronization
  1. cUrl extension installed; in order to call and obtain the information from .
  2. Define the fields relationship in the WooCommerce connector:
    1. One size for image fields.
    2. Most WooCommerce fields are already defined in each section, extra fields for products or product variants will be attributes and they must have been created in WooCommerce in order to synchronize.
    3. When synchronizing a product that has variants, WooCommerce attributes that are synchronized will be marked as Used for variations, then, attribute values from the product and product variants will be combined and assigned to the product. Variations must have only one value for each attribute.
How to synchronize by Cron
  1. To activate auto-synchronization, make sure to have an active cron job on your server.
  2. You can add or create cron jobs by adding a command on your console:
    1. * * * * * wget -q -O - https://yourdomain.com/wp-cron.php
    2. * * * * * php -q -f /path/to/your/woocommerce/root/directory/wp-cron.php

    Remember to replace yourdomain.com with your actual domain or /path/to/your/woocommerce/root/directory with the actual path of your WooCommerce installation.
